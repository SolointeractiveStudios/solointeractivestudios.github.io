Ball Run

Ball Run is a fast-paced physics-based runner where you guide a ball through winding tracks, avoid obstacles, and race to the finish.

Game Overview

In Ball Run, players control a rolling ball through increasingly challenging levels. The goal is to navigate each track, collect points, and reach the end without falling off the course.

How to Play

  • Use the arrow keys or WASD to move the ball.
  • Jump or dodge obstacles as they appear.
  • Collect bonuses for extra points and speed boosts.
  • Reach the finish line before time runs out.

Features

  • Responsive track design with smooth physics.
  • Multiple levels with unique challenges.
  • Simple controls for quick pick-up-and-play fun.
  • Score tracking and replay value.

Tips

  • Keep momentum going through bends and jumps.
  • Watch for narrow ledges and timed obstacles.
  • Use boosts carefully to avoid overshooting jumps.
